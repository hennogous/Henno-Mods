# CSC AI: progress and learnings

**Checkpoint: 1 November 2026.** Companion to the [development roadmap](csc-ai-roadmap.md)
and [confirmed objectives](csc-ai-objectives.md). This report consolidates this
thread's experiments and engine investigation; it is not a new test authorization
or a claim that the current source is release-qualified.

## Assessment

We can secure useful Quarter sites with guarded paid acquisition, leave legal
foundations for ordinary AI construction, and demonstrate several native valuation
and production controls. We also have observed functioning assisted and entirely
native chains. We have **not** established reliable, sustained supply-chain
competence across Basic CSC and RH5, or a net empire benefit from the latest package.

The main limitation is no longer simply finding Both sites. Promising sites can
lose their materials, lack the right customer geometry, wait for a Granary or
producer, or remain disabled after pillage. More completed Quarters can coexist
with fewer operating chains. Our objective remains fewer, better investments
where appropriate, measured by realised benefit rather than district count.

The latest native-placement diagnostics are closed and audited. Neither captured
the target Quarter decision; both exceeded practical callback budgets. No weights
were changed. The broad tool goal remains paused, with no active game in this
scope. Latest audited closure takes precedence over historical live/pending
sections in the linked experiment diaries.

## What has progressed

### 1. Two profiles and an installed assistance host

The intended player choice is implemented as a default-On CSC assistance setting:
Basic CSC support without RH, or automatic CSC-specific compatibility additions
when the reviewed RH version is enabled. Off permits RH without CSC tuning. A
dependent, default-On subsidy setting applies in either active profile. This
architecture has been exercised in native pilots; it is not a qualification of
every current profile, optional mod combination or multiplayer mode.

The installed CSC host owns assistance; Python observes and the reusable controller
advances the game. Purchases use AI Gold first, with an optional donation covering
only the next tile's safe-budget shortfall. Fresh quotes, ownership, reach,
treasury, reserves, caps, cooldowns and persistent one-shot receipts constrain each
step. The mechanism is **assisted city assignment plus the quoted Gold debit**,
not proven native AI PURCHASE behavior. Foundations are legal, incomplete and at
zero progress, with positive unchanged cost and queue; construction remains an
AI decision. RH's subscribed files remain read-only.

Basic-specific Builder movement, valuation and recruitment inputs must not be
confused with isolated proof that each improves chain outcomes. The RH profile
should complement RH's policy, rather than blindly duplicate its global tuning.
The [profile design](csc-ai-profiles.md) records the settings and ownership model;
its dated implementation descriptions require the later experiment evidence below.

### 2. Assistance is becoming selective and customer-aware

The initial Both-site approach successfully secured material potential, but often
placed chains with no practical route to a customer. In the selective comparison,
all four assisted sites reached working Both supply and upstream producers, yet
none operated a complete customer path by150: three lacked Stage2 customer
geometry and the fourth lacked Granary. All four also lacked registered Stage3
customer geometry. This was a concrete planning failure, not a reason to buy more
Both sites. [Selective screen](../research/mods/csc-ai-paired-selective-chain-screen-results-20261101.md).

Canonical customer-aware v2 now admits credible Base sites through Both or trial
real Production+Gold >=4 potential, separates present from projected value, and
requires an exact customer path with named prerequisites. Material and future
customer identities remain bound across purchases; unknown essential legality
fails closed. Future district promises are not treated as already realised yields
or as proof that the native AI prefers that location. The broad all-bonus-resource
private placement cue was removed because it cannot distinguish the Quarter's
materials. Its removal's marginal benefit is unproved.

One v2 assisted Bakers chain operated from119 through later observations to150.
Other admitted sites still failed through missing customers, resource removal or
pillaged producers. The planner is an executable investment mechanism, not control
over the much larger native placement cohort.
[Implementation](../research/mods/csc-ai-customer-aware-planner-v2-20261101.md),
[native pilot](../research/mods/csc-ai-customer-aware-planner-v2-native-20261101.md).

### 4. Materials: incentives can land without protecting supply

Earlier resource-Farm experiments verified conditional private80 on naturally
built Rice/Wheat/Maize/Flax Farms, with isolated terrain-only Farms receiving no
extra. At150, seven eligible resource-Farm controls and81 terrain controls passed.
Native job values/reservations and physical developments were observed, but the
combined forecast package and treatment timing prevent an isolated Farm80 efficacy
claim. Existing serialized Farms did not always acquire the new value merely
because the rows loaded; the precise attachment cause remains a hypothesis.
[Farm experiment](../research/mods/csc-ai-resource-farm150-test-20261024.md).

Direct improvement-table forecasts and conditional modifiers are different
mechanisms. Loaded Salt/Silver Mine coverage did not yield an observed positive
native Mine control; equal nominal80 does not imply equal effectiveness. Optional
resource coverage should follow enabled mappings and native improvement legality,
not guessed names or resource reclassification.

Actual repairs and new improvements occurred, but soft preservation did not
reliably prevent Cattle harvests for Farms, Banana harvest for a Mine, district
coverage, or recurring pillage. In the latest v2 case, Cattle1688 was **unimproved**
when an identified Builder harvested it for a Farm plan; no working Pasture was
destroyed and no completed replacement Farm was proved. A late absent resource
alone must not be labelled an initially bad Quarter site or a confirmed harvest.

Availability is also more than Builder count. Honey became highest in an exposed
city recommendation list at112/113 and stayed unimproved; identical lists for
different Builders did not establish assignment. Flax was absent from that exposed
list without proving illegality or zero lower-level valuation. Nearby charges,
city recruitment contracts, recommendations, reservations, travel and execution
must be evaluated separately. No blanket Builder increase follows from those
observations. [Recommendation probe](../research/mods/csc-ai-material-recommendations-20261031.md).

## Outcome checkpoints: what improved, and what did not

The earlier combined foundation/customer experiment improved several matched150
observations: all11 assisted foundations completed, mean observed delay21 turns
and median20, versus28.125/27 for eight observed prior completions. Distinct
non-CityCenter adjacent customers rose31 versus18; actual customer-building links
rose19 versus8. However, producer sites with working Base fell15 versus21. Two
inputs changed, cohorts differed and late/unknown episodes remained: this does
not isolate queue-priority efficacy. The requested200 endpoint was not completed;
controlled progress ended175 after observer death, and later uncontrolled turns
were excluded. [Audited partial result](../research/mods/csc-ai-foundation-priority200-20261025-results.md).

The more recent matched150 RH screens expose the remaining gap:

| Endpoint measure | Selective v1 | Customer-aware v2 |
|---|---:|---:|
| AI cities / population |56 /466|60 /540|
| Quarters |53|56|
| Working Base sites |19|15|
| Working Both sites |4|2|
| Functioning Stage2 producers |38|39|
| Full-supply S2-or-registered-S3 contexts |3|2|
| Assisted foundations |4|5|
| Acquisition Gold: own + subsidy |195 =81+114|200 =24+176|

Here a full-supply context requires the observed complete/unpillaged Quarter,
working owned Base, upstream producer, and actual matching customer-building path.
It is a specific operating milestone, not a measurement of total empire profit.

For v2,50 Quarters were clearly native: ten had working Base and one operated a
full context. Five were assisted: four had working Base and one operated. One
additional Quarter shared paid material acquired for another city's investment;
it had working Base but did not operate. That overlap must stay separate from a
strictly unassisted cohort. Assistance improves material coverage in this small,
selected endpoint cohort, but differing site ages/opportunities prohibit a causal
native-versus-assisted success-rate interpretation.

Of v2's200Gold,85 was committed at149 without a foundation by150: right-censored,
not a failed conversion. The pilot accepted149 AI intervals and745 research rows
without replay, but planner records11..14 were missing and sixteen original
errors were retained. Its dense-observation pass remains false. Overall v2
improvement is **not established**.

Customer links and Service flags throughout these studies are not logged monetary
transactions. Working materials and producers can deliver partial benefits before
a full-chain milestone; a nonoperating commitment is not automatically zero return.

## Native placement: a real route, an unresolved exact-material gap

We now trace ordinary adjacencies into prospective district evaluation and dynamic
normalization. Default-bias-only slices illustrate the scale: private river4
becomes5; Quarter-to-customer10 becomes12.5; customer-to-Quarter30 becomes37.5.
These are not final candidate scores. Competing-district opportunity costs,
clearance, lost land value, purchase costs, time discount and other terms remain.
The traced competing path can subtract the full maximum alternative static value,
so a large raw adjacency cue alone cannot predict the winner.

The exact neighboring mapped-material route remains unresolved. Standard resource
adjacency exposes a Boolean or one native resource class, not CSC TypeTags or an
arbitrary resource-name list. The existing Quarter-owned material40 modifier has
not been verified as a candidate-local prospective placement signal. This is not
a universal claim that the modifier always does nothing in every context. Generic
bonus-resource or Farm proxies are not acceptable evidence of exact discrimination.

Two passive observer diagnostics each advanced only1 to2. The global version
received882,774 callbacks in that interval; the local post-normalization version
received294,437, still above its preregistered100,000 continuation limit. Neither
captured the target city/Quarter decision. They closed normally with zero host
economic effects, exact installation/settings restoration and audited owner exit.
This establishes a measurement-cost limitation, not AI non-response or successful
calibration. No third retry or increased cap is implied.
[Consumer/weight evidence](../research/mods/csc-native-quarter-placement-control-evidence-20261101.md),
[full investigation](../research/mods/csc-native-quarter-placement-weights-20261101.md).

## Tooling and operating lessons

- **Use reconstructed engine evidence selectively.** The shared workbench combines
  current-build Ghidra/headless exports, reference names, assembly/PE checks,
  call graphs and targeted Frida observations. Names, prototypes and old-platform
  layouts are leads, not authoritative current Windows facts. Build-specific
  claims require the DLL hash; small aliases/merged functions need special care.
- **REA adds reusable dependency/value-trace dossiers.** It reproduced a known
  building multiplier and exposed a scripted operation setter's side effects
  before its invalid-ID check: an invalid ID is not a safe no-effect probe.
  It does not replace live decision evidence, establish token savings, or justify
  reconstructing every function. All official bundled skill files are installed;
  the REA6.8.0 MCP registration passed startup,229-tool discovery and a target-free
  session check. An existing client may need reconnect/restart. Existing runtimes
  were reused. [REA record](../research/mods/csc-ai-rea-trial-20261030.md).
- **Harden one controller.** Finite advancement, sole console ownership,
  identity-qualified processes, one-shot economic/save handling and narrowly
  guarded shipped diplomacy exits now serve probes as well as games. UI/Core
  timing differences and observer death are explicit states. A successful ACK
  is not proof of blocker release; ambiguous effects are never blindly resent.
  The latest probes exited normally: their bottleneck was callback fan-out.
- **Keep evidence compact.** Abandoned Ghidra copies were removed while active
  sessions/master data were preserved; leases and orphan cleanup guard against
  recurrence. Store small text findings and hashes, reuse source databases, and
  repair reporters offline rather than reopening games to manufacture completeness.
- **Separate evidence levels.** Definition loaded -> value delivered -> proposal
  -> selection -> operation -> completion -> retained supply -> empire benefit.
  A positive at one level does not establish the next. Cache age, instrumentation
  neutrality, sparse observations, censored episodes and discarded partial turns
  remain explicit. Join city records by player and city ID, not ID or name alone.

## Next work, in order

1. **Use existing planner records to diagnose dependency loss.** Rank missing
   customers/producers, destructive material decisions, pillage and unrealised
   future promises across native and assisted cohorts. Preserve legitimate
   alternate chains; do not force the originally planned customer if another
   valid route operates. Improve ranking and risk estimates from these cases,
   rather than widening admission to generate more purchases.
2. **Make future native observation affordable before another capture.** Demonstrate
   native-side filtering before JavaScript, with offline accepted/rejected scope
   and output-budget checks. Moving hook addresses again is insufficient. Only
   then consider one bounded, decision-relevant score/alternative capture.
3. **Calibrate a coherent mechanism, then test outcomes.** Retain working delivery
   routes; retire bounded failures. Compare native preference/cache/contract/queue
   transitions and useful completions, not only final SQL or getters. Customer
   encouragement must remain balanced against valuable ordinary district sites.
4. **Qualify both profiles over meaningful horizons.** After a justified change,
   use matched Basic/RH settings, multiple maps and enough time for eligible
   investments. Report absolute operating chains, retention/recovery, cost and
   military/economic displacement as well as per-investment efficiency. Do not
   turn reduced expenditure, more cities or Quarter spam into a success criterion.
5. **Defer strategic Service matching.** First establish placement, material care,
   balanced customers, buildings and repair; then connect reliable chains to
   player priorities and victory needs.

These are roadmap recommendations, not permission to restart a paused campaign.
The objective is unchanged; the most useful progress is converting known working
controls into reliable end-to-end outcomes without repeating uninformative tests.
