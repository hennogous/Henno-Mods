## Gate 2 — Prove one economic job after the interface gate

The preferred first new job is **improve one legal, safe, reachable mapped
material for an existing valuable chain, using an available Builder**. It tests
the missing material follow-through without mixing in new Quarter placement,
Builder production, tile purchases or multiple future investments. A repair is
a separate action path, not assumed equivalent. If static evidence instead
supports a city-bound producer request substantially better, use that single
job and state that material execution remains unresolved.

Use one fixed saved scenario and one enabled CSC goal, a declared short maximum
turn window and bounded observation. Prove the owner/city/plot/type identity at
entry and at the native consumer; include another AI with a distinct opportunity
as a negative control for cross-player leakage. Original humans remain excluded.
Inspect activation and owner gates before any recruitment side effect. Do not
copy the public combat/map-centre demonstration or force Lua movement/queue
orders to manufacture success.

Require a linked native request/reservation, acceptance or recruitment as
applicable, execution, physical completion, and release. Exercise cancellation
or expiry in a separate minimal case before integration, with a lost/invalid
target and no retry spam. No new production demand is allowed just because a
movement test failed. A callback or one move alone does not pass.

**Stop rules:** wrong/ambiguous owner, wrong target, unintended human/RH effect,
duplicate request, unbounded restart, unreleased reservation, unexplained
movement failure or the declared time/capture limit ends the attempt. Preserve
the boundary and fix only the concrete defect offline. A non-exposed economic
job is not evidence that the existing native incentives are ineffective.

**Decision:** a successful bounded lifecycle earns a small native execution
adapter, not a general AI replacement. Failure leaves an explicit unsupported
action and returns that action to the working-control route. DLL hooks/CE are
research tools; shipping a runtime dependency is a separate architecture choice.

## Gate 3 — Deliver the first useful chain decision

Keep geometry, readiness and investment value separate. Follow the confirmed
objective's current Quarter/material/customer mappings, Base/Both and real P/G
distinctions, credible future development and graded +3/+4/+5 opportunities.
Compare finishing/repairing with opening a new chain; include Gold, production,
Builder work, displaced opportunities and existing commitments. Avoid incompatible
plot promises and double-counted shared benefits. Unknown costs remain unknown.

Use the existing guarded acquisition/foundation backend where justified: fresh
legality, ownership, price and Gold checks; one-shot receipts; reassessment after
partial acquisition. Distinguish assisted placements and expenditure from native
AI choices. A geometry recommendation alone never authorizes spending.

For each next required action, use the supported native executor if proven;
otherwise use the responsive native guidance already available and observe its
actual decision. Diagnose in this order: job legality/context, capacity/access,
assignment/reservation, selection/queue competition, execution and recovery.
Increase Builder capacity only if capacity is the measured constraint. Do not
duplicate RH's existing movement/improvement policy.

The next useful tuning comparisons are driven by the first chain's failure:
producer/customer choice, improvement/harvest/repair, or customer siting against

## Compatibility and lifecycle requirements

Normal gameplay SQL/XML, registered Lua processors and exposed native-AI APIs
are the preferred shipping surface. Static C++ names, historical offsets and
Linux layouts are research evidence, not callable Lua APIs. Experimental CE or
runtime hooks must remain separate from a data/Lua implementation and its claims.

Both Basic and RH need the same CSC competence, with different adapters only
where their existing policy requires it. A new TriggeredBehaviorTrees row also
needs activation through the effective AI lists. The community gain-tech test
reports one slot per AI event and removes ordinary upgrade activation to work;
never ship that override or assume priority resolves collisions. Check effective
triggers, allowed operations and strategy lists for both profiles. Do not rely
on the deprecated Run Behavior Tree node as an unverified chaining fix.

RH5.097 already contains a scripted-target Settler Snipe operation. Its target
callback uses a global latest-target cache and a fallback scan of AIs, rather
than reading a requesting-player argument. This is useful source precedent,
not proof of caller identity or a pattern to copy. Preserve RH behavior and test
cross-AI isolation explicitly. Workshop sources remain read-only.

Any integrated goal needs a stable owner/city/target/action identity, duplicate
suppression, a finite validity window, bounded concurrent requests and bounded
resource exposure. Recheck after pillage, capture, loss of supply, changed
ownership or a conflicting native commitment; release on completion, invalidity
and cancellation. Save/reload must not duplicate requests or strand reservations.
These are adapter acceptance requirements, not a request for another framework.

Reuse NativeSession and the finite controller for probes and campaigns, with one
owner, frozen inputs, exact settings/package restoration and declared closure.
Probes use narrow event/identity filters and bounded traces; normal games remain
light. A successful zero-turn session does not certify a full-game adapter, and
millions of getter interceptions should not become routine telemetry.

## Gate 4 — Validate benefit and package Basic/RH

Screen the implemented candidate on matched saved scenarios, then a modest fixed
map set; broaden only after useful gains. Compare Basic assistance off/on, and RH
alone against RH plus CSC. Compare the revised planner with the previous policy;
use targeted ablations for uncertain contributions, then a combined interaction
test. Include subsidy-off for promising candidates, not an early combinatorial
sweep. Confirm the migrated full-game adapter in the first bounded relevant run.

Measure operating time, functioning supply/customer buildings, completed work,
material retention/repair, investment and displacement of other useful activity.
Separate own-funded from subsidised costs, native from assisted sites, present
from future benefit and late/censored chains from failures. Structural links and
Service flags are not evidence of transactions or causal empire profit.

Set acceptance bands from baselines before the comparison. Require repeated
useful follow-through without Quarter spam, idle Builders, harmful clearance,
trigger collisions or unacceptable military/economic displacement. Validate
settings, save/reload, original-human exclusion and supported compatibility.
Only supported mechanisms enter the packaged policy; cleanup of obsolete
guidance is an explicit change, not a hidden difference between test arms.

## Gate 5 — Add strategy-aware Services

Once chains can be placed, developed and recovered reliably, value Service
outcomes against observable player needs and victory direction. Adjust viable
chain choices rather than rewarding infeasible Service promises. Replan when
needs change, accounting for sunk costs without preserving every old commitment.
Different needs should produce sensible different choices and realised effects;
victory-outcome claims require longer and more varied evidence.

## Retain, defer and stop

- **Retain:** the confirmed objective, guarded backend, evidence-only evaluator/
  coordinator, shared session, observed producer/contract routes, and customer
  adjacency as a responsive but uncalibrated lever.
- **Promote:** owner-aware native economic-job feasibility, alongside the repaired
  candidate capture. Keep selection of goals distinct from execution of jobs.
- **Defer:** full producer amount calibration until decision-relevant, broad
  Builder adjustments, global weight sweeps, long campaigns and strategic
  Services until their preceding capability gates pass.
- **Stop as a working method:** exhaustive engine cataloguing, cloning dated
  runners, repeating failed setup without a specific preflight, treating Value
  magnitudes as Buildings/Districts strength, and treating sample-mod callback
  success as CSC behavior. The broad bonus-resource proxy remains a candidate
  for explicit replacement; this roadmap does not silently remove source tuning.
