## Question and evidence

Can an AI be encouraged to pursue a named civic when its owned Quarter/materials make that civic's Services useful, without forced civic selection, progress grants or free economic benefits?

Real Strategy2.3.1 uses positive Civics favored items through native strategies and reports historical Technologies favoring broken, Civics negative favoring inert. AI+v13 uses trait lists with omitted Favored/Value (current schema defaults1/0). Neither establishes that these preferences work today. Native behaviour-tree inspection motivated by AI+ found Add Goal Tech accepting Desired Civics, native contract/value fields and actual civic bindings in Build City District, Research Trigger Tech and Seek Target Wonder. How to create/withdraw a suitable goal from CSC conditions remains unknown. This is not an AI+-implemented CSC goal system.

Our previous clean comparison already removes old CSC research rows, verifies active stock/custom strategies, and covers80Online rounds. Sham/stock/custom all5002logged civic candidate records,327Naval records and400choice-observation rows per arm match. Do not repeat the same trial at higher Civics Value or longer duration without a new discriminator. No positive native-score control yet established.

- Log player target ownership, current civic/progress, actual prerequisites/researchability, target and alternative civic scores (AI_GovtPolicies.csv Civic rows), natural completion/next-selection events, callback activation, and available native tree/contract evidence. AI_Research.csv holds Tech scores, not civic scores. Do not assume the CSV final numeric field's meaning. Compare recorded score precision; no fabricated hidden scores.
- A callback/Following flag only establishes activation; rows only establish loading. A native goal must have evidence of tree execution and registration/retention where exposed; unsupported observability is an explicit limitation. Missing telemetry is unavailable, never zero effect.

Existing active research may finish normally. Preregister windows, excluded/defeated players and target eligibility, not retrospective favorable subsets.
- Score effect and behaviour effect are distinct outcomes. No score response at valid repeated opportunities rules out a demonstrated effect in that context; it does not establish universal engine inability. A score response without changed selection warrants a choice-margin/latency test, not immediate rejection.
