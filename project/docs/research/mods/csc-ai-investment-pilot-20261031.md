## Decision after Astra review

Stop hunting archives for a complete investment schema. The strict offline
reports remain useful audits, but their all-unknowns-resolved rules are not a
practical live policy. Current `review()` defers when more than one site survives,
globally prioritises any accepted unfinished commitment, and inherits uncertainty
from unrelated chains. Optional speculative customers and nine exact cost scalars
can block a good present-day action. These are limits of the offline reviewer;
they are not evidence of an engine failure or the installed host doing nothing.

Astra recommends a local, bounded decision using actual alternatives, one
reviewed action and the existing host as sole economic mutator. Begin with an
existing chain needing one useful step if one is credibly actionable; otherwise
use a low-burden Both start with working Base. Leave speculative benefits
uncredited rather than pretending their costs or timing are zero. Do not require
prior proof of native selection before testing whether native selection occurs.
