1. Two CSC-only native strategies favour Bakers/Tailors at Value300/Favored1,
   only when a confirmed assisted foundation remains incomplete in its original
   owned city, on its original plot, of its original type. Source/type mismatch,
   requested/ambiguous ledger, capture, lost city, completion, nonmajor, dead
   player or missing original-human identity fail closed. The installed role
   bridge reads the small persisted journal on native strategy evaluation;
   no map scans, production boosts, queue commands or resource/unit grants.
   Same-turn results are not cached because confirmation follows placement.
   Native strategies favour a district TYPE player-wide, not one city. Other
   placements while active are a possible side effect to measure explicitly.
2. Customer-district candidates adjacent to matching Quarters receive30 private
   planning units instead of10. Quarter candidates near customers retain10;
   City Centers remain excluded as reverse candidates, live sales tags/direct
   unique replacements remain supported. Real sales yields are unchanged.
   Private plot signals can affect human citizen allocation; no claim of fully
   inert economic behavior or isolated completion benefit.
