# Continents 250: integrated ordinary-economy guidance

**The placement guidance is producing useful Quarters and sustained Bakers
chains. Customer coordination remains the clearest weakness, especially for
Tailors.** The completed game founded 22 Quarters, all adjacent to Base materials;
20 rated +3 or better. It delivered 1,101 independently observed Storekeeper
chain-turns. Guidance processing averaged 2.03 seconds per turn across five AIs,
peaking at 4.61 seconds.

Roadmap: **1.1–1.8 and 2.1–2.2**, finding **AI-C208**. Analysis only; no game
was launched for this report. Pangaea was subsequently stopped at Henno's
request and Fractal remains unstarted. The broader three-game goal is paused.
[Compact results and provenance](csc-ai-ordinary-economy-continents-20261117.json).

### Services and continuity

Active endpoint markers: **8 Storekeepers,2 Innkeepers**. Dockmaster, Sacristan
and all Stage 4 Service groups are zero. Native Storekeeper observation covers
every turn 2–249 with no attribution ambiguity: nine sources provide 1,101 active
chain-turns; six sources exceed 100 active turns. Five interrupted chains resume
operation, with observed gaps of 4,24,6,21 and 7 turns. A final capture-related
loss remains down through the observation endpoint.

Tailors' incomplete chains have concrete spatial/investment causes:

- All four surviving Tailors have Workshops and improved Base materials, but
  none has an adjacent Harbor; there is no Stage 2 customer to develop.
- Tailors Quarter on plot 2159 has the working Temple and Workshop, but lacks its Stage 3 Tailor
  building. The other three Tailors built that producer without an adjacent
  Temple. This explains the missing Sacristan physical combination.
- Two Tailors have no mapped customer district at all. The other two have
  working Theater customers. Later ordinary transactions therefore have some
  connectivity even though Services remain absent.

Problems 1/2 should continue to improve feasible customer footprints and
coordination. Completing the missing producer/customer buildings is also a
dependency for later building guidance; it should stay explicit on the roadmap.

Nine foundations occur on previously evaluated targets. Three have a negative

Treat these as same-target follow-through observations. The receipt counts
provide the direct evidence that routine scoring adjustments reached native code.

The customer adapter skips 153 options across 11,659 forecast attempts. Its current
counter combines unavailable candidate/reference quotes and unsuitable native
raw scores. Splitting these reasons would help assess the remaining coverage.
There are 13 clamped residuals. Central joint-world work records zero budget
failures, unknown results or route-cache overflow.

## Material retention and acquisition

The 20 material-loss records comprise two resource removals, six pillage records,
five improvement-removal records and seven ownership changes. A shared Farm
can generate two affected-Quarter records. Both resource removals are non-last
Base deposits: Maize plot 914 covered by a Wonder at 48, Rice plot 1317 by a Spaceport at 195.
No recorded voluntary district placement destroys the last owned Base/Spec
deposit. Last-class losses in this record follow capture/ownership change.
The interruptions and resumed Services show why preservation and repair need
separate measurements.

There is one complete natural Quarter acquisition path: player 2 selects Tailors
1123 at 22, pays 30 Gold for it at 23, founds the Quarter at 33 and completes it at 35.
It is an admitted+2 site. This closes the purchase-to-construction loop in
ordinary play. Overall 133 measured tile payments spend 12,855 Gold, with all
debit amounts and later ownership checks matching. Category savings rejects 185
checks despite enough total treasury, confirming that earmarked funds matter.

## Priorities from this result

1. **2.1 Customer coordination:** examine suitable customer footprints around
   existing Quarters and the disconnected Tailors. Retain ordinary Harbor/Hub
   value and the shared gameplay-derived relationships.
2. **1.1–1.4 Opportunity quality:** understand which Base+Spec/+4 sites fail to
   become affordable positive offers. Current constructed sites already tie
   the best geometry in their positive city/type packets.
3. **1.5–1.6 Follow-through:** retain the successful savings/payment/construction
   path and use the remaining seeds to test its frequency.
4. **2.2 Retention:** preserve the distinction between non-last development
   tradeoffs, improvement disruption and territorial losses. Track continued
   recovery in subsequent games.
5. **G5 Performance:** profile repeated world/native pricing and refresh cadence
   while keeping changed materials, investments and native eligibility fresh.
