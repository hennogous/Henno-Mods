## Native rejection loops explain part of the cost

Cattle2533 had one-charge Builder327683 on it25. Native UnitOperations repeatedly
records Can't Start for HARVEST_RESOURCE and BUILD_IMPROVEMENT21..26 while the
same-player/unit/turn tree reserves the Cattle for a Farm. The tree's Clear Plot
and Build Improvement nodes report SUCCESS. Those labels are bookkeeping, not
proof that a native operation executed or an improvement appeared.

The loop resolved: the first Pasture reservation is27, and native Builder589829
starts BUILD_IMPROVEMENT at the exact17,34 coordinates28, matching the care
transition develop→working. This was a different Builder. The endpoint Pasture
confirms development. A short rejected episode must not be called permanent idle.

Non-CSC Stone1868 also drew rejected Farm/harvest work43..49. It is still Stone
without an improvement75. At50 a Builder stood there with three charges; that
Builder is absent from the75 census, which does not prove death or continuous
idling. The coarse rule affects useful non-CSC work as well as CSC materials.

There are87 rejected-work log records in the rule arm versus65 in control;
seven versus three routes have rejections on multiple observed turns. These are
log records and conservative player/unit/plot/operation routes, not unique
requests, hidden job identities or a causal accounting of every wasted turn.
BuildImprovement parameters are not logged here; Farm/Pasture intent comes from
the associated tree, not an invented native request payload.

## Consequences for guidance

1. Treat preservation, material improvement and producer/customer functioning as
   separate gates. Retaining an unimproved Sheep or Cattle is incomplete success.
2. Keep native legality and native effects separate from behavior-tree statuses.
   Capture rejected action paths and whether they resolve into valid development.
3. Do not ship a player-wide harvest ban as targeted material care. It prevents
   destructive actions, but also removes resource plots from Quarter eligibility
   and permits repeated requests from still-selected incompatible jobs.
4. The next test should examine choosing the suitable improvement earlier. An
   uninstalled same-source16 Pasture forecast diagnostic adds80 private planning
   yield directly in native Improvement_YieldChanges, with harvesting allowed.
   It tests a fresh native forecast representation rather than more generic
   Builder demand. Positive results would still require targeting and calibration.

No new world is needed. Preparation is schema-checked and idempotent, with real
resource/improvement yield rows unchanged and no observed Pastures at source16.
It is not installed, driver-ready or natively tested. Before launch, preserve the
ended418 source pins, wire a distinct declared branch, test its exact native DB/
manifest/default source gates, and retain canonical native play/transport/planner.
