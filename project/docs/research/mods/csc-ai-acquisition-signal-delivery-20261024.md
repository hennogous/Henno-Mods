## Consequences for guidance and metrics

The acquisition ownership/Gold mechanism works in this control. Its newly bought
land does not exhibit the same UI private-property response as already-owned
land. This does not yet establish the AI task valuation or preservation response.
The Gameplay plot getter also fails the positive control, so calling its zeros a
native AI failure would be unsupported. The existing native Pasture job-choice
and physical development evidence remains distinct from this property test.

Keep Core/UI domains, before/after initialization phases and reload boundaries
explicit. The new reusable `material_signal_delivery.py` classifies incomplete,
uncontrolled, matched, absent and mixed responses without turning missing data
into zeros or AI consumption claims. Six focused checks include the actual native
audit and counterexamples.

Next measure the same contrasts through native city-level private yields with
worked-plot and ordinary-input controls. If that getter is also insensitive before
advancement, a separately declared bounded native-turn observation is needed;
do not infer that an inactive-turn cache equals the AI's planning score. Do not
reattach modifiers, spoof native events or relax the75 source gate to force a pass.
Any change must have a tested reusable implementation and isolated native proof.
The175 mature follow-through, subsequent material/decision analysis and overnight
synthesis remain pending. This is not completion or a deployment recommendation.
