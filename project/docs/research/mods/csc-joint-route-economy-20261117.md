## Implemented path

refreshes endpoint buildings/properties in Gameplay and qualifies trader start
times. Stale/future packets and changed operations cannot enter the joint world.
Only definitions persist beyond the publication.

`EconomyFacts.TradeWorld` turns the same central sites into plain route events.
Native requirements own material classes/counts. Each predicate becomes ready
with its kth distinct supplier; all predicates must hold. Current improved
suppliers need no worker or registered material-input host. Future jobs reuse
central material ETAs. A missing producer is keyed to the chosen shared building
investment. Quarter foundation/repair changes the origin import-blocking flag
at foundation/repair time, independently of future producer construction.

`EconomyModel.RouteLedger` runs once after investment scheduling and before
pricing. It includes foreign contributors to local totals and prices only the
evaluating player's receipts. A route-only world needs no existing Quarter.
Vanilla route yields and Services remain excluded.

Supplier displacement currently supports immediate foundation-time removal only.
Future-dated removal is rejected; it needs an explicit pre/post-removal supply
timeline before that broader scenario can be valued.

## Coverage is distinct from removal

Astra caught earliest-end eligibility being treated as expiration. Removing one
uncertain contributor can invent modulo32 recovery. The corrected kernel keeps
contributors and stops quoting the entire affected counter group when coverage
ends. Only an explicit known-removal event can establish32→31 recovery.

Beyond coverage, route amenity adjustment is neutral against current native city
happiness: no forecast route income and no invented amenity loss. Other ordinary
future amenities can still be quoted against current city state. This is an
explicit bounded valuation assumption; future route selection/renewal is deferred.
