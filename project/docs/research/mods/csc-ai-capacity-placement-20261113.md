## Candidate

The planner reads the stock UI population-district counters once per city per
publication. Its existing Gameplay district walk counts unfinished and pillaged
population-limited claims, excluding internal and exempt districts. Population
and occupied-count disagreement makes the record unknown. Available capacity
allows the existing quality/economic forecast; full or unknown capacity defers
new positive Quarter offers and retains the managed negative default. Every new
snapshot reconsiders the city, so growth can reopen offers. Existing Quarter
foundations and repairs bypass the new-slot branch.

The allowed counter includes city-specific extra allowances. Matching population
and inventory is a partial freshness check: it cannot independently detect a stale
extra allowance. Native admission remains authoritative at consumption. The gate
does not force a build, hardcode population/3, remove future unlocks/frontier sites,
or alter material-protection deductions for other districts.
