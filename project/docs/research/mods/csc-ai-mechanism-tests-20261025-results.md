# CSC AI mechanism probes — results, 25 October 2026

Seven matched turn-100→105 outcome arms are complete and independently audited.
They establish responsive native valuation channels, but do not establish a
finished AI policy or long-term supply-chain improvement. The final prospective
preservation pair is **unobserved**, not a negative result: the host powered off
before its control loaded; a newer gameplay/art build subsequently replaced the
test installation. Closeout preserved that build rather than overwriting it.
No further native work or recurring monitor is pending for this probe suite.

## 1. Customer adjacency is a working valuation lever

There are140 positive prospective site getter controls. Removing or tripling the
private customer bonus changes those values exactly, while real adjacency is
unchanged. At the initial capture100, city advice remained cached. After the next
AI planning interval, capture101 showed different recommendation sets in15 studied
cities for zero and16 for30. Native production-contract and behavior-tree scores
also changed. The physical placement set through105 remained the same as baseline.

This separates immediate native valuation from planning refresh and actual
construction. It supports retaining a carefully calibrated customer incentive;
it does not establish that30 is balanced or better than10.

Coimbra exposes a legal Quarter-adjacent Harbor site1266 with real Gold0/private10
(or0/30), versus same-city alternatives1194 with real Gold3/private0,1195 Gold2,
1193 Gold1 and1196 Gold0. However, none of the three arms logged a Coimbra Harbor
trigger through104: it was not a completed site-choice test. A qualifying same-city
adjacent option versus a superior **+4** Harbor was not observed. There were other
+4 sites elsewhere, which are not valid substitutes for that comparison.

Candidate tie/conflict counts in the exposure file are opportunities, not unique
jobs or decisions. Prospective getter values cannot establish a regret cap, and
private30 cannot be equated numerically with real Gold30.

## 2. The broad bonus-resource proxy affects native siting

Fifty-nine isolated prospective controls confirm removal of the generic12 signal
while river4 and actual economic adjacency remain. Native outcomes change.

Classifications use first-observed owned supply, not exact decision-time facts.
Newly acquired plots are included. Neither does not mean economically worthless:
Quarter exchanges and customer interactions may still matter. The proxy-off arm
substituted one site and omitted another, rather than uniformly improving the
remaining sites. Both arms placed two new non-CityCenter customer districts.
Constructible-building additions were9 versus10; the extra was a Monument, not a
new CSC producer. A different Workshop proposal is not physical completion.

The proxy is therefore **effective but poorly targeted**, rather than inert. It
is a defensible removal/replacement candidate for the redesign because it rewards
unrelated bonus resources; this short result alone does not prove better economy,
chain density or retention after removal. No shipping cleanup was applied here.

## 3. Building-production routing remains unresolved

At100, six legal unbuilt buildings had positive private forecasts: Granaries40,
CSC Workshops80 or120. There were also92 other sampled legal unbuilt ordinary
opportunities, including seven vanilla Workshops with real Production3.

Despite that exposure, the private specialization and the ordinary offset control
both exactly matched baseline's490 scoped production records,28 strategy records
and32,089 behavior-tree records, recommendation captures and physical plot changes.
The intended positive control failed to produce a response. Consequently this
does not prove that custom specializations are unsupported, that all building
forecasts are ignored, or that longer observation would be identical. Input
loading was proven; meaningful consumption of this tested route was not.

Do not simply increase the private forecast again or ship the diagnostic10000
offset. A future test must first establish a responsive shipped-route control,
actual adoption and fresh eligible selection opportunities.

## 4. Material values land, but preservation is not proven

Two attempts to turn existing material modifier amounts to0 did not yield a
valid behavioral ablation. The first caught a validator column-index mistake;
the second loaded exactly the42 intended zero amounts, but existing plot values
did not change. It stopped before explicit AI advancement. Saved-instance binding
or caching is a hypothesis, not an isolated cause. Loaded DB rows are insufficient
evidence that an intervention changed the native value being tested.

The fresh-attachment arm solved the **delivery test**, without changing real plot
yields during on/off/restored calibration. It exposed29 unique plots:15 lastBase,
9 uniqueSpec and5 redundant. Native city/Builder scores changed. That is not proof
of a direct marginal forecast on every changed Builder job: city effects can
spill into unrelated jobs and citizen allocation.

No better material retention or repair was demonstrated through105. Rice1087
remained unrepaired; native new development already occurred in baseline (three
supporting Farms and one Plantation). Deer2386 was removed in both arms; the
treatment arm additionally had a Farm there by105. This is not preservation.

The prominent Maize1863 loss was **already committed before treatment**. Original
logs at99 show Nuri contract2012 for a Tailors Quarter, Value8198, Reserve Plot
(13,25)=1863 and accepted production. The neighboring assisted Bakers1862 was
founded99; material purchase98 cost60=5 AI Gold+55 fallback. Source100 treatment
therefore cannot test avoidance of that earlier decision. We must not interpret
its continued construction as a clean rejection of preservation incentives.

## Timing and observation windows

DB definitions are available on game load; effect application to existing saved
instances is a separate question. A new modifier or property gate can update the
native getter immediately, as the fresh attachment controls demonstrate. Cached
advice, strategy adoption/withdrawal, reservations, production queues and actual
work have their own timing. Earlier campaign evidence observed a12-turn strategy
withdrawal lag; it is not a universal constant.

Five turns were sufficient to observe a response from customer and bonus-resource
signals, but are insufficient to reject all lagging mechanisms, measure producer
completion, repair response or retained supply. For later validation:

- Separate input loading → actual value → adoption → first fresh proposal/target
  → selection → physical completion → retention.
- Begin preservation before the competing commitment and verify that timing in
  logs. Waiting longer after an irrevocable reservation does not fix exposure.
- Give completion cohorts enough age, using observed delays (previous RH assisted
  foundations took roughly20–30 turns on average/median, with censoring). A
  suggested longer window is test design, not a guaranteed native deadline.
- Observe repair/development over repeated actionable opportunities; retain cases
  with no Builder/path/safety exposure as unobserved rather than scorer failures.
- Use a responsive same-path positive control before interpreting non-response.

## Implications for the next design

The reliable present lever is native district adjacency valuation. Use it to
encourage specific chain benefits without claiming that it selects the correct
site or preserves materials automatically. The generic bonus proxy demonstrably
steers siting and should not stand in for exact mapped supply. Building selection,
fresh preservation and sensible real-adjacency trade-offs still require resolved
mechanism/decision exposure before they can be called working.

Proceed next to the agreed fewer/better chain design: one combined Both-or-+4 real
Production+Gold policy with credible future development, explicit material and
customer opportunity costs, and measured completion/retention. This report does
not specify or implement that planner, remove shared guidance, raise Builder
biases, modify RH files, or establish Service payments. Customer-building links
and Service flags remain distinct from unobserved actual payments.
