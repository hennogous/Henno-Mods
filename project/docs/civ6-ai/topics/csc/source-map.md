- Current City Center population consumers differ from older producer-owned definitions. A historical game did not execute today's whole source tree.

- Both potential, district-free supply, working improvements, producer/customer milestone, Service activation and benefit differ. Partial benefits precede the full milestone.
- Spending 195 versus 795 with 3 versus 4 full contexts is not success. V2's individual operating chain coexists with 2 versus 3 combined contexts.
- Latest human stops and audited closure override historical ACTIVE/pending diary language. No old game, campaign or monitor is an active job here.
