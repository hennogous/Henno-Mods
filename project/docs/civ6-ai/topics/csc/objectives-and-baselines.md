# Objectives, baselines and success

The [6 November objectives](../../../integrations/csc-ai-objectives.md) cover all
eight Quarter families and their shared assets. The [roadmap](../../roadmap.md)
starts with one working Bakers Stage 2 chain. The results below establish
the research baselines and limits for measuring that work.

```mermaid
flowchart LR
 O[Opportunity] --> P[Site chosen] --> Q[Quarter complete] --> M[Materials developed] --> C[Producer and customer] --> W[Working chain over time] --> B[Empire benefit]
```

## AI-C001 — The objective is empire benefit

CSC AI should use worthwhile, durable supply chains to support empire needs, priorities and victory progress. Fewer good chains may beat more weak commitments. Both Basic and RH profiles share this objective; strategic Service selection remains an eventual capability.

Scope: Confirmed project direction, 28 October, through the latest closed studies.

Limits: District counts, subsidies, Service flags and isolated functioning chains do not establish the full objective. Sustained multi-map Basic/RH competence and net benefit remain unproved.

Sources: [Objectives](../../../integrations/csc-ai-objectives.md), [progress](../../../integrations/csc-ai-progress-and-learnings-20261101.md).

## AI-C002 — The true baseline excludes CSC AI aids

The agreed baseline retains real CSC gameplay and only the shared resource-generation pass. It excludes private AI cues, Builder tuning, research guidance, paid acquisition and injected foundations. Disabling an external planner alone does not create it. RH-only is a separate underlying-AI reference, not stock AI.

Scope: The formal comparison contract accepted 22 October.

Limits: Historical tuned controls, saved-world counterfactuals and old resource-pass versions must retain their actual labels. Verify initial maps and loaded actions, not seeds alone.

Sources: [Benchmark contract](../../../research/mods/csc-ai-benchmark-metrics-20261022.md), [profiles](../../../integrations/csc-ai-profiles.md).

## AI-C003 — Early baselines exposed distinct bottlenecks

Five Standard-speed turn 200 games contained 175 major-city observations and 188 completed Quarters, only five with both owned material classes. Bakers had 130 mills,14 Bakeries and no Cafes; Tailors had four Workshops. The later ten Online 250 games contained 598 major-city observations and 776 Quarters, only six retaining Both. These are different clocks and samples.

Scope: Five October 15 Standard games and ten October 16–17 Online vanilla/TAR games.

Limits: Descriptive baselines are not causal tests. Owned plot-legal alternatives were scanned up to one turn earlier and did not establish affordability or complete production eligibility. Tailor Both-choice denominators were particularly small.

Sources: [Original baseline](../../../research/mods/csc-ai-baseline-20261015.md), [paired Online baseline](../../../research/mods/csc-ai-paired-standard-20261016.md).

## AI-C004 — Both potential is not working supply

Base can support Stage 2/3 Services; Both is especially valuable for Stage 4 potential. Ownership and adjacency do not prove valid improvements, producers, customers or timely operation. Ordinary sales can operate without material or Service-civic gates, and partial material/producer benefits can precede a full-chain milestone.

Scope: Mechanic-specific measurements across historical experiments and November source.

Limits: A nonoperating full-supply commitment is not automatically zero return or waste. Structural links and Service properties are not logged monetary payments.

Sources: [Objectives](../../../integrations/csc-ai-objectives.md), [sales reconstruction](../../../research/mods/csc-ai-customer-comparison-20261023.md), [current forecast study](../../../research/mods/csc-future-economy-forecast-20261103.md).

## AI-C005 — Measure conversion, durability and trade-offs

Use fixed opportunity definitions, absolute operating counts and city/population context. Separate native/assisted origins, site ages, own funds/subsidies, losses, repairs and supply-turns. Compare overall empire development and displaced investment as well as conversion efficiency.

Scope: Common evaluation framework, rather than a single profit formula.

Limits: Games are independent sampling units; five players from one map are not five worlds. Late investments are censored, zero opportunities are not zero success, and missing telemetry remains unknown.

Sources: [Benchmark metrics](../../../research/mods/csc-ai-benchmark-metrics-20261022.md), [objectives](../../../integrations/csc-ai-objectives.md).
