# Current CSC AI status

Updated 11 November 2026. Active scope: **Problems 1.1–1.8 and 2.1–2.2**
in the [numbered roadmap](roadmap.md). The goal remains open.

## What we can demonstrate

- Packaged CSC Lua and our CE development fork deliver bounded district/site preferences to native comparisons. Individual examples changed native choices and requests; earlier controlled cases also reached Quarter completion.
- The site planner considers known materials, projected local Mill adjacency, mapped customers across stages and enabled City Lights providers. It can credit one feasible future neighbor. These are planning estimates, not assured future construction.
- A matched Nicopolis test showed one weak-site deduction changing the native winner. The frontier extension also changed a native choice before ownership. Neither test established a better completed Quarter at those alternative sites.
- Earlier matched cases demonstrated customer-building guidance, supplier protection and recovery of a Bakers chain. Their scope does not prove every Quarter, selector or later planning cycle.

## Active problem: 1.5, acquiring a worthwhile site

The latest exact replay followed Nicopolis's accepted frontier request through
turn 216. The site stayed unowned and empty. Eleven Economic attempts obtained
valid city quotes, but the earmarked plot fund remained below the native
threshold. Savings accumulated while the quote changed. Actual Treasury and
net income were not captured by that native observer.

All 121 purchase observations and two district selections matched their streamed
journals exactly. The controller verified normal exit, preference restoration
and temporary-package retirement. Astra independently reviewed the decoding,
fund arithmetic and limits. See [AI-C141](findings.md#ai-c141) and its evidence.

The next test needs a frontier footprint with **known credible Base supply,
a concrete customer path and a positive quote**. Discover it in existing saves;
then observe Treasury/income separately from earmarked funds, paid acquisition,
reservation and construction. A supplier deposit outside the border is a separate
purchase problem. The Nicopolis site still has an unrevealed neighbor and cannot
qualify as an improved chain opportunity.

## What remains open

The roadmap's sub-problems need individual native evidence. Broad placement
quality, dependable poor-site avoidance, city deferral/reconsideration,
footprint and supplier acquisition, and repeated cross-quarter follow-through
remain unqualified in the full intended scope. A delivered score or accepted
request does not close construction or realised-benefit requirements.

The Stage 2 objective remains useful follow-through context. Current work
systematically qualifies placement capabilities first, with stock game AI;
RHAI5 compatibility is deferred. No upstream CE PR is authorized yet.
