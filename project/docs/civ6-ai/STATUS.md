# Current CSC AI status

Updated 11 November 2026. Active scope: **Problems 1.1–1.8 and 2.1–2.2**
in the [numbered roadmap](roadmap.md). The goal remains open.

## What we can demonstrate

- Packaged CSC Lua and our CE development fork deliver bounded district/site preferences to native comparisons. Individual examples changed native choices and requests; earlier controlled cases also reached Quarter completion.
- The site planner considers known materials, projected local Mill adjacency, mapped customers across stages and enabled City Lights providers. It can credit one feasible future neighbor. These are planning estimates, not assured future construction.
- A matched Nicopolis test showed one weak-site deduction changing the native winner. The frontier extension also changed a native choice before ownership. Neither test established a better completed Quarter at those alternative sites.
- Earlier matched cases demonstrated customer-building guidance, supplier protection and recovery of a Bakers chain. Their scope does not prove every Quarter, selector or later planning cycle.

## Active problem: 1.5, acquiring a worthwhile site

The main mod now publishes bounded positive frontier offers using the same
site model and +24/+48 bands as owned sites. Nine exact-pair CE receipts across
three pairs confirmed staging; eight confirmed score writes. The summed
citySelections counter was two, with no site wins. This proves delivery, not
purchase or better placement. [AI-C147](findings.md#ai-c147).

The next dependency is **buying for the intended city**. Szeged and Debrecen
both quote 85 Gold for Szeged's prospective plot. The native purchase node can
accept same-player ownership, but reservation requires the original city's
ownership. The preflight stopped before play. The current quote guard avoids
ambiguous buyers; it cannot bind a retained contract's eventual buyer.
[AI-C145](findings.md#ai-c145).

We now have a verified native link from city to new district tree, initialized
purchase contract and later purchase attempt. The six-turn Nicopolis replay
linked city 524295 to contract 4333 and target 401; all 24 receipts matched
the stream and cleanup verified. That target still lacked cash and remained
unowned. Nearby 398 became owned through an unresolved route.
[AI-C146](findings.md#ai-c146).

The required-buyer prototype's read-only run verified both selector listeners
against the same Neighborhood decision. It did not establish active CSC
publication: the older untuned checkpoint lacks the early identity-capture
path, and the watcher had no initialized Base tags. This is not a frontier
offer losing. [AI-C148](findings.md#ai-c148).

The explicit fixture migration passed those gates. Szeged's target received
eight native writes and eligible cache reads. It gained exactly the offered
6,144 Q8, but Neighborhood still won; no target contract appeared. This is
delivery evidence, not acquisition. [AI-C149](findings.md#ai-c149).

Next compare Nuremberg's frontier 862 with owned 788, and Hamburg's frontier
1011 with its owned alternatives. Establish an economic reason to buy before
testing the required buyer. Preserve native prices, savings and payment;
follow debit, ownership and foundation.
Reload/lifecycle support and the maintainable CE API remain open. Supplier
acquisition is a separate 1.5 branch. No buyer override has run live yet.

quality, dependable poor-site avoidance, city deferral/reconsideration,
footprint and supplier acquisition, and repeated cross-quarter follow-through

request does not close construction or realised-benefit requirements.

systematically qualifies placement capabilities first, with stock game AI;
RHAI5 compatibility is deferred. No upstream CE PR is authorized yet.
