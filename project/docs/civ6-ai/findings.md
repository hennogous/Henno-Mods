## AI-C001

**The objective is empire benefit**

Status: accepted. Evidence: source.

CSC AI should use worthwhile, durable supply chains to support empire needs, priorities and victory progress. Fewer good chains may beat more weak commitments. Both Basic and RH profiles share this objective; strategic Service selection remains an eventual capability.

**Scope:** Confirmed project direction, 28 October, through the latest closed studies.

**Limits:** District counts, subsidies, Service flags and isolated functioning chains do not establish the full objective. Sustained multi-map Basic/RH competence and net benefit remain unproved.

## AI-C002

**The true baseline excludes CSC AI aids**

Status: accepted. Evidence: source.

The agreed baseline retains real CSC gameplay and only the shared resource-generation pass. It excludes private AI cues, Builder tuning, research guidance, paid acquisition and injected foundations. Disabling an external planner alone does not create it. RH-only is a separate underlying-AI reference, not stock AI.

**Scope:** The formal comparison contract accepted 22 October.

**Limits:** Historical tuned controls, saved-world counterfactuals and old resource-pass versions must retain their actual labels. Verify initial maps and loaded actions, not seeds alone.

## AI-C003

**Early baselines exposed distinct bottlenecks**

Status: accepted. Evidence: runtime.

Five Standard-speed turn 200 games contained 175 major-city observations and 188 completed Quarters, only five with both owned material classes. Bakers had 130 mills,14 Bakeries and no Cafes; Tailors had four Workshops. The later ten Online 250 games contained 598 major-city observations and 776 Quarters, only six retaining Both. These are different clocks and samples.

**Scope:** Five October 15 Standard games and ten October 16–17 Online vanilla/TAR games.

**Limits:** Descriptive baselines are not causal tests. Owned plot-legal alternatives were scanned up to one turn earlier and did not establish affordability or complete production eligibility. Tailor Both-choice denominators were particularly small.

## AI-C004

**Both potential is not working supply**

Status: accepted. Evidence: source, runtime.

Base can support Stage 2/3 Services; Both is especially valuable for Stage 4 potential. Ownership and adjacency do not prove valid improvements, producers, customers or timely operation. Ordinary sales can operate without material or Service-civic gates, and partial material/producer benefits can precede a full-chain milestone.

**Scope:** Mechanic-specific measurements across historical experiments and November source.

**Limits:** A nonoperating full-supply commitment is not automatically zero return or waste. Structural links and Service properties are not logged monetary payments.

## AI-C005

**Measure conversion, durability and trade-offs**

Status: accepted. Evidence: source.

Use fixed opportunity definitions, absolute operating counts and city/population context. Separate native/assisted origins, site ages, own funds/subsidies, losses, repairs and supply-turns. Compare overall empire development and displaced investment as well as conversion efficiency.

**Scope:** Common evaluation framework, rather than a single profit formula.

**Limits:** Games are independent sampling units; five players from one map are not five worlds. Late investments are censored, zero opportunities are not zero success, and missing telemetry remains unknown.

## AI-C006

**More resources do not ensure useful city opportunities**

Status: accepted. Evidence: source, runtime.

Fifteen paired native maps showed TAR Abundant mostly redistributing sites, while Truly Abundant increased Both-site counts. Some paired maps lacked Tailor specialty resources entirely. Offline studies and native helper follow-ups developed a resource-generation pass; production scope narrowed to Bakers/Tailors and temporary Tailor density assistance increased opportunities.

**Scope:** Five paired landscapes, historical 80-layout emulator study and separate native helper/density samples.

**Limits:** Geographic counts precede ownership, city reach, visibility, research and complete legality. Fixed-landscape emulation does not reproduce all native RNG/start adjustment. Shared clusters are not independent investments, and map assistance is not AI competence.

## AI-C008

**The broad BONUS cue was effective but poorly targeted**

Status: accepted. Evidence: source, runtime.

The generic bonus-resource proxy changed native values and siting, but rewarded resources unmapped to the relevant Quarter. In density-v 4, nine of eleven Tailors beside bonus resources had only wrong-industry bonus types. Later mechanism contrasts confirmed responsiveness without a demonstrated chain-quality improvement; current source removes BONUS 12.

**Scope:** Historical proxy experiments and current November source.

**Limits:** Removing it is not itself proved to improve the economy. Broader guidance can incidentally help, so removal and exact-material replacement require their own outcome evidence.

## AI-C009

**Markers and resource-class sets were rejected representations**

Status: accepted. Evidence: runtime.

Synthetic feature markers reached prospective native previews but accumulated clearing, placement and presentation side effects. A separate custom resource-class probe worked, while tested list encodings behaved as single distinct class identifiers. Resource reclassification would change gameplay and cannot cleanly represent overlapping CSC TypeTags.

**Scope:** October 17 marker trial and October 28 native resource-class probes.

**Limits:** Preview recognition does not establish safe metadata or better natural choices. The tested class encodings failed multiple membership; do not infer arbitrary parser capabilities from a TEXT column.

## AI-C011

**Broad Builder tuning had mixed confounded outcomes**

Status: accepted. Evidence: runtime, static.

The historical 90/900 package reached improvement density 7.76/city versus 5.22 at the common 200 checkpoint, but did not finish 250. A later resource-layout variant with the same settings gave 6.70/city at 200. Training, purchases, unit additions and remaining charges measure different things. Later native tracing established that the Units-list Value adds to request priority rather than acting like a percentage; Basic's Builder 50 is not a promise of 50 workers. Required City=-1 removes one city-ID restriction while retaining ordinary eligibility checks; it does not assign a worker or guarantee recruitment.

**Scope:** One-seed historical Builder/density trials and current-build static Units/request consumer tracing.

**Limits:** Interrupted runs do not enter completed 250 comparisons. Combined settings and changed worlds do not identify a useful individual coefficient or prove sufficient workers for a particular job.

## AI-C012

**Private yields are not human-economically inert**

Status: accepted. Evidence: runtime.

**Scope:** All-owner raw-resource/improvement mechanism arms.

**Limits:** No direct conversion into money does not imply no economic effect. Older inert-output wording is superseded; all-owner diagnostic packages were not AI-only release candidates.

## AI-C013

**Soft care landed without reliable preservation**

Status: accepted. Evidence: runtime.

Material-care v 1 stopped 170; v 2 ended after observer defeat 192 rather than 225. V 2 recorded eleven repair-to-working episodes and thirteen supporting-link losses, including four confirmed native harvests for Farm jobs. A working improvement was sometimes actively removed before harvesting.

**Scope:** Historical 160/80/20 care and urgency experiments.

**Limits:** Occupancy alone is not a confirmed harvest. Repairs do not prove incentive causality, and nodeSUCCESS is not operation/completion. Neither endpoint achieved the declared 225 test.

## AI-C014

**A harvest ban was an unsuitable preservation control**

Status: accepted. Evidence: runtime.

The no-harvest rule prevented the raw control’s three losses through 75 and yielded six versus five working Base producers. It also removed Quarter options and caused repeated rejected harvest/build routes, some later adapting to Pastures.

**Scope:** Identical source 16→75 counterfactual pair.

**Limits:** One world, shared private signals and subsidies; zero working Both producers. A player-wide legality restriction is not targeted care and was not promoted.

## AI-C016
