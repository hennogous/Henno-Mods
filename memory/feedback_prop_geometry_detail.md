# Prop detail belongs in paint — 13 October 2026

Henno, during Fashion House coach production: "remember that we don't need the
small details as geometry, e.g. around the seat".

Use geometry for the main masses, silhouette, actual openings and essential
structural support. Carry fine trim, gilding, small borders and seat ornament in
the atlas. A vertex budget is a ceiling, not permission to fill it with tiny rods
or strips. Apply this proactively to later props, even when spare vertices remain.

Coach response: remove the two thin seat rails, two door-frame rods and both
roof-edge strips; keep the seat/cushion masses, wheels, door, lanterns, shafts
and step supports. Re-bake self-AO after this geometry change.
