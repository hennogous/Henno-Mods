# AI test-game reporting and unattended evidence

Henno, 3 November 2026: during this and future automated Civ test games, a
high-level check-in every **15 minutes** is sufficient. Do not poll the human
for routine per-turn approval or narrate each turn. Report meaningful failures,
requests for needed input and the completed result promptly.

The shared controller must remain responsible for bounded play and presentation
recovery. Drivers must leave durable evidence of start/configuration, original
player identities, code/content/dependency pins, every scan/decision/published
policy window, actions or score changes, errors and the last completed boundary.
Record the declared stop condition and actual termination/restoration separately.
An interrupted active interval is partial; do not invent unrecorded events or
claim that a last complete scan proves a later interval had no effects.

The full-adjacency pilot already writes per-turn scan/plan/native artifacts,
operation intents, timestamped command journals, source archives and process/
preference closure receipts. A consolidated timeline should make them readable
without another game. Preserve the original preferences across a machine restart.
